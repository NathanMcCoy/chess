    Two players on one device. Tap a piece, then where it goes, or drag it.

    How Gliński’s hexagonal chess differs
    • Rooks move straight out through a cell’s six sides; bishops through its six corners, so each side has three bishops, one for each colour of cell.
    • The queen does both, the king one step either way, and the knight jumps to the 12 nearest cells that aren’t on one of the queen’s lines.
    • Pawns move straight up the file and capture one cell forward at 60°. From their starting cells they may move two. They promote on the last cell of their file.
    • There’s no castling. Stalemate isn’t an even draw: the side that gives it scores ¾, the other ¼.
